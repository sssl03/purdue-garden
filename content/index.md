last semester, i encouraged graduate students to make-in-public during their capstone projects. i invited [[arjun-itp]] to introduce the class to obsidian–quartz, but students mostly used notion or wix (etc) for their logs. i don't recall any student publicising their log, but them writing _something_—even when done poorly—helped me guide them better. 

while reflecting on his own *garden*, arjun wrote so: "i realized _\[sic\]_ the importance of writing (and sharing) in public. i would have random conversations with people, just because they read a thought that i posted on this blog; or see someone figure out an electronic-component by going through my experiments-log." i wish his observations infect some of my students at [[purdue university]]. in part, this is why i am getting back to writing a public log after a long break away: so that i may practice and not just preach.
