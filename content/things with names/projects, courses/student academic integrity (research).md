research project taken up by [[jacqui kane]], [[missy casner]], and me from summer 2026, where we try and collect examples of student dishonesty from different instructors (eg. faculty, lecturers, exam proctors) in our department. this is not to paint students in poor light : this is so that we can better understand the different ways in which dishonesty expresses itself, and then hopefully encourage informed discussions around the topic. — 20260926
