research project taken up by [[jacqui kane]], [[missy casner]], and me from summer 2026, where we try and collect examples of student academic dishonesty from different instructors (eg. faculty, lecturers, exam proctors) in our department. this is not to paint students in poor light : this is so that we can better understand the different reasons why and manners in which students are driven to be dishonest, and then hopefully encourage well-informed discussions around the topic. — 20260926
