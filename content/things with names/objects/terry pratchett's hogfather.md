a live-action adaptation of one of terry pratchett's novels. the film came into my life in 2019, when [[jennifer haugan]] recommended it to me. the first time i watched it[^2] i wasn't as enthralled by it, but i kept coming back to it, and it rapidly grew on me. the film culminates in an exchange between death and susan about belief ; i consider that piece of writing[^1] a work of genius.

[^1]: because it was a piece of writing before it was made into a film.

[^2]: i still remember where i was when i watched it: lazing on a couch in a large living room in a former swedish soldier's apartment in reimersholme, stockholm.
