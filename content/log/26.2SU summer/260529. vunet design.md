discussion with the design team at [[vunet]] about how to structure design work. 

they'd stopped using figma, and were only using cursor for all their design work. there was some good in their current approach, but not using a canvas-based tool had some side-effects too. so, i spoke with them about how to break design work into discrete steps (and choose specific tools for each step, instead of trying to use a single tool for everything), how to keep their work structured, and how to maintain versions (of code-based and visual work, both). also encouraged them to maintain the design-team's focus on "stories", because without stories the product function in a company suffers, because different teams (engineering, development, sales, marketing, etc) warp stories for each feature in their different ways whenever a feature passes through their hands.
